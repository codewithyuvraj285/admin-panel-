ABOUT PARQHOur story

PARQH is a premium handmade custom clothing brand and designer boutique in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. We blend traditional artistry with contemporary fashion, creating custom ethnic and modern outfits for men and women, with a curated range of premium ready-made collections, including Sahu community attire.

From everyday elegance to festive and ceremonial wear, every piece is built on fabric quality, precision tailoring, refined detailing and a perfect fit.
